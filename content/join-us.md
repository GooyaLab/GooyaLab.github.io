## Open Positions

We are always looking for passionate and talented people to join our research team. If you are excited about machine learning for medical imaging and want your work to make a difference to patients, we would love to hear from you!

**Open positions include PhD students, visiting researchers and postdoctoral researchers.**

### PhD Positions

<ul class="fund"><li>Scholarship-funded and self-funded positions</li></ul>
<div class="indent">

We are seeking PhD students in **deep learning, probabilistic and generative modelling** for medical imaging and patient metadata.

Apply through the University of Glasgow postgraduate research application system: <a href="https://www.gla.ac.uk/postgraduate/research/computingscience/" target="_blank">Apply Here</a>, and mention **Dr Ali Gooya** as your proposed supervisor.

Funding opportunities: <a href="https://www.gla.ac.uk/scholarships/" target="_blank">University of Glasgow scholarships</a> (including School of Computing Science and China Scholarship Council schemes).

<span class="ok">What we look for:</span> a strong background in computer science, engineering, mathematics or physics; experience in deep learning; ideally first-authored papers in Q1 journals or top venues such as MICCAI, IPMI or CVPR; and good English communication.

</div>

<ul class="fund"><li>Doctoral Training Programme (DTP) positions</li></ul>
<div class="indent">

Doctoral Training Programmes may fund both UK and international students, but each programme has its own rules and deadlines. When a DTP project with our lab is open, it will be announced on the <a href="/">home page</a> with a direct application link.

</div>

### Visiting PhD Students and Researchers

We welcome self-motivated visiting PhD students and researchers who share our research interests. To apply, please contact the PI directly with your CV and a short research proposal.

### Postdoctoral Positions

Postdoctoral vacancies are advertised on <a href="https://www.gla.ac.uk/explore/jobs/" target="_blank">University of Glasgow Jobs</a>. We are also happy to support strong candidates applying for independent fellowships (e.g. Marie Skłodowska-Curie, Royal Society, Leverhulme).
